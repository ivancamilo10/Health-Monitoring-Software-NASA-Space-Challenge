Clinical SBAR Handover & DSN Uplink

Aerospace SBAR Clinical Protocol

S · Situation: Active flight contingency, clinical triage state, and deviating vitals.
B · Background: Mission phase (ISS/Moon/Mars), career dose (mSv), and crew baseline.
A · Assessment: Multi-hazard synergistic correlation and clinical etiology.
R · Recommendation: Immediate crew countermeasures, medication steps, and flight surgeon review.

Cryptographic CRC32 Integrity

Deep space communications through the NASA Deep Space Network (DSN) suffer from noise and packet dropouts. Telemetry packets are timestamped and cryptographically validated:

DSN-PACKET: VANCE-01-20261115-SPE
PROTOCOL: NASA DSN Ka-Band (32 GHz)
CHECKSUM: CRC32(0x9AF83B2E) [VERIFIED]
LATENCY: 24 ms (ISS) | 1.3 s (Moon) | 22 min (Mars)
When the crew transmits data back to Earth, it is formatted using the hospital gold standard: SBAR (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation). Packets include CRC32 checksums so Houston flight surgeons receive mathematically verified data without transmission corruption.
