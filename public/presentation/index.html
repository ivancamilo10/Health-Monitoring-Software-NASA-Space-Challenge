  • NASA-STD-3001 Vol 1 & 2: Permissible operational health thresholds for blood pressure, core temperature, and cardiac rhythm.
  • NASA HRP Risk Matrix: Acute radiation events (SPE), cardiovascular deconditioning, confinement stress, and SANS.
  • NASA PVT (Psychomotor Vigilance Test): Real-time detection of cognitive lapses and operational neuromuscular fatigue.
  • Radiation Career Limit: Continuous monitoring against NASA's 600 mSv permissible lifetime dose standard.

High-Fidelity 3D Anatomical Twin

Integration of Open-Source BodyParts3D and Three.js

Real 3D Human Skeleton (BodyParts3D)

Rigorous polygonal mesh decoding of a complete human skeleton (cranium, cervical/thoracic spine, ribcage, and pelvic girdle) adapted from the open-source repository ivancamilo10/mitina.

The frosted translucent astronaut suit eliminates intrusive wireframe mesh lines, ensuring an unobstructed view of internal organs and clinical hotspots.

Real Systolic Anatomical 3D Heart

Procedural vascular construction complete with the aortic arch, three supra-aortic vessels, pulmonary trunk, and left anterior descending (LAD) coronary arteries.

Dynamic mathematical ventricular torsion and systolic contraction pulsation synchronized in real time with the astronaut's live heart rate.

SBAR Clinical Handover & DSN Uplink

Structured Aerospace Handover with Cryptographic Integrity

Standard Hospital SBAR Protocol

S (Situation): Active contingency, triage state, and critical physiological deviations.
B (Background): Mission context (ISS/Moon/Mars), accumulated career dose, and baseline values.
A (Assessment): Multi-hazard synergistic breakdown and clinical root causes.
R (Recommendation): Immediate crew countermeasures, prescribed countermeasures, and pharmacological steps.
